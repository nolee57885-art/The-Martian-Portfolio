The Martian's Pursuits

Mars is often cast as Earth's unruly red child — fierce, formidable, and brimming with boundless energy. It embodies the will to persevere: to rise after failure, to keep pushing when the work is difficult, and to carry on when the path ahead is uncertain.

Energy, however immense, finds its purpose in direction. Guided by intent, it can build, create, and transform.

The Martian's Pursuits is me stepping into my energy and giving it flow.

02 / The through-line

Strength is not the absence of vulnerability.

It is the willingness to learn, adapt, and keep moving. Every skill, setback, and experience becomes part of the whole.

Eight years of practice have given me a foundation. They have not given me every answer — and that is exactly why curiosity still matters.

01 / Clinical practice

Skill lives in the details.

My clinical work brings together hands-on practice, careful reasoning, and an appreciation for cases that do not arrive with simple answers. Manual therapy, soft-tissue techniques, and ischaemic compression are part of the toolkit; judgement is in knowing how and when to use them.

02 / Learning in motion

Stay a student.

Study and continuing professional development are not boxes to tick. They are how I challenge what I think I know, make room for better questions, and keep my practice moving forward.

03 / The personal experiment

Research meets real life.

My own fitness journey has included the struggle to stay consistent and to make nutrition work in everyday life. It has pushed me to explore training approaches relevant to my body, my context, and the realities of being an Indian woman — while remembering that no single plan fits everyone.

04 / Sport-specific thinking

Movement has a context.

Sport-specific work means looking beyond a symptom to the demands of the activity: its movement patterns, loads, skills, and the person's goals. The aim is to keep the individual, not just the injury, at the centre of the process.

05 / Building something of my own

Make the idea real.

Building a business independently asks for a different kind of endurance: planning, making decisions with incomplete information, learning from missteps, and returning to the work. It is another practice in turning energy into direction.

06 / A little code, a lot of curiosity

Try, break, understand, repeat.

Coding is a growing corner of my world — a place to experiment with structure, solve small problems, and learn by making. It is not the whole story, just another way I like to explore.
