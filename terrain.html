01 / Build the foundation

The Terrain:
Find Your Footing

Before speed, power, or performance, there is a foundation worth building.

I.
Groundwork

Build a body that has somewhere to go.

Every meaningful progression begins with the basics: the ability to produce force, access the movement you need, and control the positions you move through. The foundation is rarely flashy. It is what makes the next step possible.

01

Strength

Developing muscular capacity through appropriately progressed resistance work. Strength gives movement a dependable base and helps prepare the body for the demands placed on it.

02

Flexibility

Exploring and developing the range of motion relevant to the person and the task—not flexibility for its own sake, but range that has a purpose.

03

Mobility & control

Pairing available range with coordination, stability, and control. The question becomes not only “Can I reach this position?” but “Can I move into and out of it with intention?”

A foundation is not a limitation. It is the ground from which possibility grows.

Training choices and progression depend on individual goals, capacity, symptoms, and context. This page describes a general approach to strength and conditioning, not a universal prescription.
